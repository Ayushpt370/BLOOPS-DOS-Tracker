    Which way should I send?
    • Copy emails for BCC: one email to everyone. Paste the addresses into BCC and use the generic preview below. Fastest.
    • Mail-merge CSV: personalized subject and body for each person. Works with Gmail add-ons like YAMM or GMass, or Word/Outlook mail merge.
    • Open on a row: opens a ready-to-send email to one person in Gmail. Good for VIPs and sponsors you want to personalize further.

          How the likelihood score works
          
      Each organization is rated 0–5 on five factors, weighted into a score out of 100:
      
          
        
            
      • Connection to B4P (30%): existing partnership or U-M ties, plus live alumni links. Every alum in your donor list whose company matches adds a point (up to 3), and one more if an alum there is senior or works in philanthropy. Import more people and scores update on their own.
        • 
            
      • Mission fit (25%): health, medical supplies, global health or sustainability.
        • 
            
      • Giving track record (20%): known grants, sponsorships or matching-gift programs.
        • 
            
      • Ease of asking (15%): open application or clear contact, compared with invitation-only.
        • 
            
      • Timing (10%): can realistically give by Giving Tuesday.
        • 
          
      
          
      Click any organization to see its alumni, sources and contact, and to adjust the factors.
